# Riders at a Glance

| Product | Primary focus | Benefit or trigger noted | Waiting period noted |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| WP | Total and permanent disability | Waives eligible main-policy premiums; source lists before age 60 and 180 days | Policy definition applies |
| WPCI | Critical illness | Waives eligible future main-policy premiums; 40 severe illnesses in source | 60 Days |
| Health Happy | Medical expenses | 1M-25M annual limits; selected 2x benefit and plan-specific sublimits | 30 days general illness; 120 days special diseases |
| CI Plus | Severe critical illness | One 100% SA benefit for qualifying illness or death; 44 conditions in source | 60 Days |
| Health Cancer | Cancer | Per-unit death benefit and daily inpatient benefit; source lists up to 500 days | 30 Days |

## Quick Recall

- WP = disability-related premium waiver.
- WPCI = critical-illness premium waiver.
- CI Plus = one-time critical-illness payout.
- Health Cancer = cancer-focused cover with daily hospital income.
- Health Happy = medical expense cover with several annual-limit options.

This table summarizes study notes only. It is not a substitute for policy wording.
