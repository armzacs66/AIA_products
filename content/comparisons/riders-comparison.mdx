# Riders at a Glance

| Product | Primary focus | Benefit or trigger noted | Waiting period noted |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| WP | Total and permanent disability | Waives future premiums; disability before age 60 | Not stated in this note |
| WPCI | Critical illness | Waives future main policy premiums; 40 severe critical illnesses | 60 Days |
| Health Happy | Medical expenses | Annual-limit plans; selected benefits for 6 critical illnesses | 30 days general illness; 120 days special diseases |
| CI Plus | Severe critical illness | One-time lump-sum payout; 44 illnesses | 60 Days |
| Health Cancer | Cancer | Death benefit and daily hospital income | 30 Days |

## Quick Recall

- WP = disability-related premium waiver.
- WPCI = critical-illness premium waiver.
- CI Plus = one-time critical-illness payout.
- Health Cancer = cancer-focused cover with daily hospital income.
- Health Happy = medical expense cover with several annual-limit options.

This table summarizes study notes only. It is not a substitute for policy wording.
