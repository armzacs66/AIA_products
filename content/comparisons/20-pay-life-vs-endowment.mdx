# Comparison

| Feature | 20 Pay Life | Endowment 15/25 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Type | Whole Life | Endowment |
| Premium | 20 Years | 15 Years |
| Coverage | To age 99 | 25 Years |
| Cash Return | No annual cash return listed | 1% annually in years 2-24 |
| Maturity Benefit | 100% SA at age 99, subject to terms | 121% SA in year 25; stated total 144% including annual returns |
| Death Benefit | 100% SA, subject to terms | Steps from 100% to 135% by policy year |
| Premium Structure | Main-policy premium paid for 20 years | Level premium paid for 15 years |
| Rider Term | Main policy lasts to age 99 | Main policy ends after 25 years; attached riders may end with it |
| Tax Benefit | Subject to current rules and combined limits | Subject to current rules and limited to eligible premiums paid during the 15-year term |
| Legacy Planning | Excellent fit | Moderate fit |
| Savings Goal | Lower fit | Higher fit |

## Recommendation

### Estate Planning

Consider AIA 20 Pay Life.

### Savings + Tax Planning

Consider AIA Endowment 15/25.

Review current product documents and personal circumstances before making a decision.
