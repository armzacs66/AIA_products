# CI Plus Exam Traps

| Situation | In these study notes |
| --- | --- |
| Invasive Cancer | Covered |
| Stage 0 Cancer | Not covered |
| Stroke | Covered |
| TIA | Not covered |
| CABG | Covered |
| Balloon | Not covered |
| Stent | Not covered |
| Open Valve Surgery | Covered |
| TAVI | Not covered |

## Waiting Period

60 Days

## HIV

Not covered, with these listed exceptions:

- HIV from blood transfusion
- Occupational HIV

## More Claim Traps

- The study source describes one critical-illness benefit or a death benefit, not daily hospital income or reimbursement of treatment expenses.
- Child critical illnesses are a separate group; the source lists coverage before age 17, subject to the policy definition.
- Stroke definitions require more than a brief symptom: the source highlights persistent neurological impairment and excludes TIA.
- Heart-procedure examples distinguish qualifying surgery from angioplasty, stents, and TAVI.
- The source lists pre-existing conditions and intentional self-injury among exclusions. Some disease definitions also have their own age limits.

## Practice Check

1. What is the listed waiting period? **60 days.**
2. Is TIA treated as a covered stroke example in these notes? **No.**
3. Does CI Plus pay daily hospital income? **No; the source describes a one-time benefit.**
4. How many juvenile critical illnesses are listed? **Four, with the source noting an age limit before 17.**

Always check the current policy definition and exclusions before relying on these examples.
