# Health Happy Exam Traps

| Topic | Key fact |
| --- | --- |
| Plan annual limits | 1M, 5M, 15M, and 25M |
| General illness waiting period | 30 Days |
| Special disease waiting period | 120 Days |
| Special disease examples | Hernia, cataract, tonsils, endometriosis |
| Double benefit | 2x annual limit for 4 policy years for 6 critical illnesses |
| PMCM availability | 15M and 25M plans |
| ICU | Actual expense |
| Double-limit conditions | 6 specified critical illnesses; up to 2x the annual limit for 4 policy years |
| Take-home medicine | THB 20,000 per visit for 1M/5M/15M; actual expense for 25M |
| OPD | Listed only for 25M: THB 2,000 per visit, up to 30 visits/year |

The six double-limit conditions listed in the study source are invasive cancer, acute myocardial infarction, stroke, CABG, organ or bone-marrow transplantation, and aortic surgery.

## Quick Check

- Do not confuse the 30-day general illness period with the 120-day special disease period.
- PMCM is listed for the 15M and 25M plans only.
- The double benefit is limited to 6 critical illnesses and 4 policy years in these notes.
- Surgery/procedures and specified dialysis/cancer treatments are described as actual-expense benefits subject to the annual maximum.
- The source describes possible 30% copayment at renewal when its stated conditions are met, including at least 3 simple-disease claims and a claims ratio of at least 200%.

Confirm the definitions, sublimits, renewal terms, and conditions in current official materials.
