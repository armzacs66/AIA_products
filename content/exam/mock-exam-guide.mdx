# Mock Exam Guide

Try answering before opening the answer key. These practice questions use only the facts summarized in this library.

## Questions

1. In the 20 Pay Life name, how many years is the premium payment period in these notes?
2. For Endowment 15/25, how long is the premium period and how long is coverage?
3. Which waiver rider is associated with total and permanent disability?
4. What waiting period is listed for WPCI?
5. What are the Health Happy waiting periods for general illness and special diseases?
6. How long is the listed waiting period for CI Plus?
7. How many annual-limit choices are listed for Health Happy, and what are they?
8. For Endowment 15/25, what is the stated total maturity return including annual cash returns?
9. Which policy years have the Endowment 15/25 annual cash return?
10. How is interest set if an Endowment 15/25 cash return is left with the company?
11. How many days is the Health Cancer waiting period, and what does one unit list?
12. What is the Health Happy take-home medicine benefit shown for the 25M plan?

## Answer Key

1. 20 years.
2. 15 years of premium payments and 25 years of coverage.
3. WP.
4. 60 days.
5. 30 days for general illness and 120 days for special diseases.
6. 60 days.
7. Four choices: 1M, 5M, 15M, and 25M.
8. 144% of the starting sum assured in total: 23 annual returns of 1% plus a 121% maturity benefit, according to the study source.
9. Policy years 2-24.
10. At the rate announced by the company; the source does not describe it as fixed for the full term.
11. 30 days; one unit lists THB 100,000 death benefit and THB 1,000 daily inpatient benefit, subject to policy terms.
12. Actual expense, subject to the plan's policy limits.

## Study Method

After checking an answer, revisit the product note and explain the fact in your own words. Verify coverage questions against current official policy wording.
