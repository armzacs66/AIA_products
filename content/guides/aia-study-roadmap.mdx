# AIA Study Roadmap

Use this sequence to build familiarity with the products covered in this library. Adjust the pace to your exam date and training materials.

## 1. Learn the Main Policies

Review [20 Pay Life](/products/20-pay-life) and [Endowment 15/25](/products/endowment-15-25). Focus on the difference between premium payment period and coverage period.

## 2. Separate Medical and Critical Illness Cover

Read [Health Happy](/products/health-happy), [CI Plus](/products/ci-plus), and [Health Cancer](/products/health-cancer). Compare their purpose, waiting periods, and benefit styles in [CI Plus vs Health Cancer](/comparisons/ci-plus-vs-health-cancer).

## 3. Memorize Waiver Rider Shortcuts

Review [WP](/products/wp) and [WPCI](/products/wpci), then use the [riders comparison](/comparisons/riders-comparison) to check the distinction.

## 4. Practice Retrieval

Work through [CI Plus Exam Traps](/exam/ci-plus-exam-traps), [Health Happy Exam Traps](/exam/health-happy-exam-traps), and the [Mock Exam Guide](/exam/mock-exam-guide). Revisit any question you missed.

## Final Review

Use the [Product Decision Tree](/guides/decision-tree) to organize products by purpose. Confirm all exam answers against the current course materials and official policy documents.
