## Double Benefit

The study source lists six qualifying critical illnesses: invasive cancer, acute myocardial infarction, stroke, CABG, organ or bone-marrow transplantation, and aortic surgery. The annual maximum may increase to 2x for up to 4 policy years when the policy definition and benefit conditions are met.

## Additional Covered-Service Notes

- The source lists day surgery, diagnostic tests up to 30 days before or after an inpatient stay, and targeted cancer therapy, subject to policy conditions.
- It describes an AIA Vitality bonus of up to 15% of standard premium for eligible participants. Program terms can change.

## Eligibility and Renewal Notes

- The source lists issue ages of 11-75 and renewal through age 98, subject to the main policy remaining in force.
- Premiums may increase by age band. The source describes a possible 30% copayment at renewal when specified claims-frequency and loss-ratio conditions are met, including at least 3 simple-disease claims and a claims ratio of at least 200%.
- The rider must be attached to an eligible AIA main policy; coverage cannot be assumed to outlast that policy.
- The source lists health-insurance tax relief up to THB 25,000 per year, subject to the combined THB 100,000 life-insurance limit and current tax rules.

## Exclusion and Claim Notes

The source groups common exclusions/restrictions as pre-existing conditions; congenital or genetic conditions; cosmetic, anti-aging, and weight-loss care; pregnancy, childbirth, and fertility treatment; routine dental and vision care; mental-health, substance-use, and sleep-apnea treatment; self-inflicted injury, crime, and war; and experimental or non-medically necessary treatment. It mentions exceptions for some cases, but does not reproduce the full policy wording.

The source also mentions possible exceptions for certain congenital conditions after the policy has been in force for a specified period and symptoms appear after age 16, molar-pregnancy cancer, and emergency dental injury caused by an accident. It says HIV, alcohol-related accidents, hazardous sports, terrorism, and radiation are not among its summarized general exclusions. These statements do not confirm coverage; special terms, endorsements, and the full contract still apply.

Hospital admission must meet medical-necessity requirements. Accident claims are described as covered from the first day; illness waiting periods and all exclusions remain subject to the policy.

Treat these as study notes, not a complete benefit schedule. Exact sublimits, exclusions, renewal terms, waiting periods, and copayment rules are governed by the current policy and schedule.
