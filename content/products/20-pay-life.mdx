# AIA 20 Pay Life (Non Par)

## Overview

Whole Life Insurance.

## Quick Summary

| Policy item | Study-source summary |
| --- | --- |
| Policy type | Main policy; whole life; Non-Par (no dividends) |
| Premium payment | 20 years; level main-policy premium in the source |
| Coverage | To age 99 |
| Death benefit | 100% of sum assured, subject to policy terms |
| Maturity benefit | 100% of sum assured at age 99, subject to policy terms |
| Minimum sum assured | THB 100,000 in the source |
| Issue age | Source material varies: 15 days/1 month to age 70; confirm the exact policy version |
| Cash returns | No annual cash return is listed |

### Key Features

- Premium Payment: 20 Years
- Coverage: Until Age 99
- Death Benefit: 100% Sum Assured
- Maturity Benefit: 100% Sum Assured
- Tax Deductible

## Best Use Cases

- Legacy Planning
- Estate Transfer
- Long-term Protection
- Base Policy for Riders

## Advantages

- Pay short, cover long
- Suitable for attaching health riders
- Fixed premium structure

## Limitations

- No annual cash return
- Not designed as a savings product

## Additional Details from the Study Source

- Non-Par means the policy does not participate in company profits through dividends.
- Medical examinations depend on issue age, sum assured, and underwriting rules; answer application questions accurately.
- Early surrender value follows the policy schedule and may be less than premiums paid, especially in the early years.
- Premiums for attached health or critical-illness riders are separate and may change with age or renewal terms; they do not necessarily end after the 20-year main-policy payment period.
- The source describes life-insurance tax relief as subject to Revenue Department rules and the applicable annual limit, combined with other eligible life policies.

Check the issued policy and current official product documents for exact eligibility, benefits, rider terms, and tax treatment.
