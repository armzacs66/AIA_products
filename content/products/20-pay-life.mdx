# AIA 20 Pay Life (Non Par)

## Overview

Whole Life Insurance.

### Key Features

- Premium Payment: 20 Years
- Coverage: Until Age 99
- Death Benefit: 100% Sum Assured
- Maturity Benefit: 100% Sum Assured
- Tax Deductible

## Best Use Cases

- Legacy Planning
- Estate Transfer
- Long-term Protection
- Base Policy for Riders

## Advantages

- Pay short, cover long
- Suitable for attaching health riders
- Fixed premium structure

## Limitations

- No annual cash return
- Not designed as a savings product
