# WPCI

## Definition

Premium waiver rider for Critical Illness.

## Coverage

40 Severe Critical Illnesses

## Waiting Period

60 Days

## Quick Summary

| Policy item | Study-source summary |
| --- | --- |
| Trigger | First diagnosis of a qualifying severe critical illness while alive |
| Covered illnesses | 40 severe critical illnesses in the source |
| Waiting period | 60 days |
| Premium waived | Eligible future premiums for the main policy, through its premium-payment term |
| WPCI premium | Described as level during its payment term |
| Other riders | May continue if their own premiums are paid |
| Source-listed issue age | 16-75 years; confirm the current policy version |
| Tax | Source says WPCI premium is not eligible for personal income-tax relief |

## Benefits

- After a qualifying critical-illness claim, waives eligible future premiums for the main policy, as described in the study source.
- The source describes a fixed WPCI premium during its payment term and says other riders may continue if their premiums are paid separately.
- WPCI is distinct from CI Plus: WPCI waives premiums; CI Plus pays a critical-illness benefit.
- The source says WPCI is an optional rider, with premium payable separately from the main policy.
- The source describes waiver beginning with the next premium installment and continuing through the end of the main policy's premium-payment term.

## Exam Shortcut

- WP = Disability
