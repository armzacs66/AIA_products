# WPCI

## Definition

Premium waiver rider for Critical Illness.

## Coverage

40 Severe Critical Illnesses

## Waiting Period

60 Days

## Benefits

- Waives future main policy premiums

## Exam Shortcut

- WPCI = Critical Illness
- WP = Disability
