# Waiver of Premium (WP)

## Definition

Premium waiver rider for Total and Permanent Disability.

## Conditions

- Permanent disability
- Before age 60

## Benefits

- Waives eligible future premiums of the main policy after a qualifying disability claim.
- The study source describes total and permanent disability before age 60, with disability lasting at least 180 days under the policy definition.
- WP does not generally waive premiums for other riders. The source cautions that some attached riders may end when the waiver is approved; check the relevant contract.

## Exam Shortcut

WP = Disability

The trigger, waiting requirements, covered premium, and effect on attached riders depend on the policy wording and product version.
