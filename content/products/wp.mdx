# Waiver of Premium (WP)

## Definition

Premium waiver rider for Total and Permanent Disability.

## Conditions

- Permanent disability
- Before age 60

## Quick Summary

| Policy item | Study-source summary |
| --- | --- |
| Trigger | Total and permanent disability, subject to the policy definition |
| Age condition | Disability before age 60 in the source |
| Duration condition | At least 180 days in the source; confirm the actual definition |
| Premium waived | Eligible premiums of the main policy only |
| Main policy | Remains active after an approved waiver |
| Other riders | Not automatically included in the waiver; some may end under their own terms |
| Availability | May be included with some plans, subject to plan limits; source cites a THB 4 million cap for Endowment 15/25 |

## Benefits

- Waives eligible future premiums of the main policy after a qualifying disability claim.
- The study source describes total and permanent disability before age 60, with disability lasting at least 180 days under the policy definition.
- WP does not generally waive premiums for other riders. The source cautions that some attached riders may end when the waiver is approved and unused premiums may be refunded; check the relevant contract.
- The source describes WP as potentially included with some main policies rather than always requiring a separate purchase.
- Premiums may qualify for tax relief under the applicable tax rules; confirm eligibility with current Revenue Department guidance.

## Exam Shortcut

WP = Disability

The trigger, waiting requirements, covered premium, and effect on attached riders depend on the policy wording and product version.
