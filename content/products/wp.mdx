# Waiver of Premium (WP)

## Definition

Premium waiver rider for Total and Permanent Disability.

## Conditions

- Permanent disability
- Before age 60

## Benefits

- Waives future premiums
- Main policy remains active

## Exam Shortcut

WP = Disability
