# AIA CI Plus

## Product Type

Critical Illness Rider

## Coverage Style

Lump Sum; find and pay.

## Core Benefits

- 44 Severe Critical Illnesses
- Death Benefit Included
- One-Time Payout

## Waiting Period

60 Days

## Coverage Age

Up to Age 99

## Claim Notes from the Study Source

- The source describes a one-time benefit for a qualifying severe critical illness, or a death benefit, according to the policy terms; it is not hospital-expense reimbursement or daily hospital income.
- Examples of claim distinctions in the source: invasive cancer versus in-situ/stage 0 cancer; qualifying stroke versus transient ischemic attack (TIA); and specified open-heart procedures versus balloon angioplasty, stenting, or TAVI.
- The source lists exclusions that include the 60-day waiting period, pre-existing conditions, suicide or intentional self-injury, and HIV except for specified blood-transfusion or occupational exposures.
- Some condition definitions have age limits; the source, for example, identifies child conditions as limited to before age 17. Exact condition definitions and age limits vary by policy wording.

Use the policy definitions and exclusions—not a short example list—to assess eligibility for a claim.
