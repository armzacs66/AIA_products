# AIA Endowment 15/25

Savings-oriented endowment plan.

## Policy Structure

| Item | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Pay Premium | 15 Years |
| Coverage | 25 Years |
| Cash Return | 1% of starting sum assured each year in years 2-24 (23 payments) |
| Maturity Benefit | 121% of starting sum assured at year 25 |
| Total if held to maturity | 144% of starting sum assured, including all listed cash returns |
| Death Benefit | 100% in years 1-11; 110% year 12; 115% year 13; 125% year 14; 135% years 15-25 |
| Source-listed issue age / minimum sum assured | 1 month-65 years / THB 100,000 |

## Tax Benefit

Eligible for life insurance tax deduction.

## Best Use Cases

- Tax planning
- Savings discipline
- Medium-term financial goals

## Exam Notes

- 15 = Premium Period
- 25 = Coverage Period

## Additional Benefits in the Study Source

### Maturity and Death Benefits

- The 1% annual cash return is listed for policy years 2-24: 23 payments in total.
- The stated maturity benefit is 121% of the starting sum assured in policy year 25; together with the 23 cash returns, the stated total is 144% if the policy reaches maturity.
- The source lists stepped death benefits: 100% in years 1-11, 110% in year 12, 115% in year 13, 125% in year 14, and 135% in years 15-25.
- If annual returns are left with the company, interest is based on the rate announced by the company and is not a fixed 1% or 1.25% for the whole term.

### Eligibility and Premium Notes

- The source lists an issue age of 1 month to 65 years and a minimum sum assured of THB 100,000; underwriting and product-version rules apply.
- It lists premium discounts from a sum assured of THB 300,000: THB 1 per THB 1,000 for THB 300,000-599,999, and THB 1.50 per THB 1,000 from THB 600,000.
- The source describes a 31-day grace period. Confirm the applicable grace period and all benefit calculations in the issued policy.
- WP may be included subject to company limits; the study source cites a maximum of THB 4 million per life. Confirm the policy schedule.
- This is a main policy and the source says it can be bought without first owning a whole-life policy; eligible health, critical-illness, accident, income, and waiver riders may be attached subject to underwriting.
- The main policy ends after 25 years, so attached riders cannot be assumed to continue beyond the main policy term.
- The source says life-insurance tax relief applies to premiums actually paid during the 15-year premium term, subject to tax rules and the annual limit.

For illustration only, the source estimates an annual premium of about THB 80,500 at age 35 for THB 1,000,000 sum assured (THB 1,207,500 over 15 years). It lists THB 10,000 annual returns for 23 years and THB 1,210,000 at maturity. This is not a current quote or a personal recommendation.

The source lists payment by credit card, mobile banking/QR through AIA+, or automatic bank debit; availability, installment terms, and promotions vary. Annual cash returns may be paid out, retained with the company at its announced interest rate, or—where the policy allows—used toward a later premium. The source also lists a 31-day premium grace period; verify the contract.
