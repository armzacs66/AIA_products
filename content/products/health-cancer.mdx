# AIA Health Cancer

## Coverage

Cancer-specific rider.

## Benefits

### Death Benefit

Available.

### Hospital Daily Benefit

Available.

## Unit Structure

1 unit provides:

- 100,000 THB death benefit
- 1,000 THB daily hospital income
- Daily inpatient benefit is subject to the policy definition and the source lists a maximum of 500 days.

## Waiting Period

30 Days

The study source describes the death benefit as linked to a qualifying critical illness, not as general accidental-death cover. Exact covered conditions, inpatient requirements, benefit limits, and claim definitions are set by the current policy.

## Best Use Cases

- Cancer protection
- Daily income replacement
- Supplemental CI coverage
