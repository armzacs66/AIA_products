# AIA Health Cancer

## Quick Summary

| Policy item | Study-source summary |
| --- | --- |
| Policy type | Cancer-focused supplementary rider |
| Benefit structure | Per-unit critical-illness death benefit plus daily inpatient cash benefit |
| One unit | THB 100,000 death benefit and THB 1,000 per eligible inpatient day |
| Daily benefit limit | Source lists up to 500 days |
| Waiting period | 30 days from the rider's effective date; source also mentions the latest reinstatement date |
| Key distinction | Not general medical-expense reimbursement or general accidental-death cover |

## Coverage

Cancer-specific rider.

## Benefits

The source describes two main benefits for a qualifying cancer/critical-illness event:

- A death benefit, with the amount determined by the number of units.
- A daily cash benefit for eligible inpatient hospitalization, with the source listing a maximum of 500 days.

## Unit Structure

| Benefit per unit | Amount in the study source |
| --- | ---: |
| Critical-illness death benefit | THB 100,000 |
| Eligible inpatient daily benefit | THB 1,000/day |
| Maximum daily-benefit days | 500 days |

The source refers to seven serious cancer conditions but does not enumerate their names. Check the current policy's covered-condition definitions and any survival, inpatient, or claim requirements.

## Waiting Period

30 Days

The death benefit described in the source is linked to a qualifying critical illness, not general accidental death. The daily benefit is cash income for eligible inpatient stays, not reimbursement of medical bills. Exact covered conditions, inpatient requirements, benefit limits, and claim definitions are set by the current policy.

## Best Use Cases

- Cancer protection
- Daily income replacement
- Supplemental CI coverage
